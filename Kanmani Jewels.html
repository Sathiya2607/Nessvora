Our Story
◈

Kanmani Covering Jewels was born in 2009 in the heart of Salem, Tamil Nadu, with a simple dream — to make the beauty of traditional South Indian gold jewellery accessible to every woman.

Founded by Smt. Kanagarani Devi, a passionate lover of traditional Tamil jewellery, our brand has grown from a small shop in Gandhi Nagar to serving over 50,000 customers across India.

Every piece carries the soul of Tamil Nadu's rich jewellery-making tradition — from temple-inspired designs to intricate Thanjavur work — now available at a fraction of the cost of solid gold.

Care Instructions

Simple tips to keep your Kanmani jewellery shining for years

💧
Avoid Water Contact
Remove jewellery before bathing, swimming, or washing dishes. Water exposure reduces the lifespan of gold plating significantly.
🧴
No Chemicals
Keep perfumes, deodorants, hairsprays away from your jewellery. Apply them first, then wear your pieces.
🧺
Proper Storage
Store each piece separately in a zip-lock pouch or soft cloth bag. Keep in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
🧼
Cleaning
Gently wipe with a soft, dry cloth after each use. For stubborn stains, use a soft brush with mild soap, dry immediately.
🏃
Remove Before Activities
Take off jewellery before exercise, cooking, or sleeping to avoid bending, breaking, or tarnishing.
✨
Re-polishing
Contact us for re-polishing services. We can restore most pieces to their original shine for a nominal fee.
